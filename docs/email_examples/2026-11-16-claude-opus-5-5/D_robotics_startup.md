# D_robotics_startup

Request: Fais-moi un CV pour un stage Robotics Engineer chez une startup à Singapour.

Situation: spontaneous · story line: robotics · organisation facts: none (must not be described)

## Strategy (model answers)

- **why_organisation**: No organisation facts are verified, so the reason is the place and the field: a robotics team in Singapore, where he wants to work abroad and see how automation reaches its users.
- **why_role**: A robotics engineering internship lets him apply robotics training to real production equipment instead of only simulations.
- **why_me**: He has built a robotic cell's digital shadow and worked on a test bench and on engineering data workflows inside industrial companies.
- **differentiator**: He is a robotics-track engineer whose experience comes from real industrial settings, not only lab projects.
- **action**: Ask whether a team could host an intern from April to August 2027 and make it easy to forward the message to the right person.
